04 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

Good questions.

What should I prepare for our first conversation?

A few photos, the project city, and a short list of what you want to change are a useful start. If you have material ideas or timing preferences, share those too. You do not need a finished design to contact us.

What affects the quote?

The scope, existing conditions, cabinetry and surface choices, layout changes, and installation requirements all matter. We review the work and selections before developing a project-specific quote.

How do material selections work?

We review material options with you. For labor-and-materials projects, your selections inform the quote. For labor-only projects, you select and supply materials; we discuss any choices that change the installation work or labor cost.

Can we discuss changing the layout?

Yes. Tell us what is not working about the current kitchen. We can discuss layout updates and the construction scope. When a project needs an architect or interior designer, we coordinate with external professionals; we do not present them as an in-house team.

What happens after I contact you?

We start by discussing your goals, project location, and the work you have in mind. We then agree on what information or site review is needed next. Sending an inquiry does not reserve a start date or book an appointment.

Can we start before every material is selected?

Yes. Share your priorities and the decisions still open. We can discuss options and the information needed to define the scope; material selections and installation requirements need to be clarified for the quote.
