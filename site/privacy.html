WEBSITE PRIVACY

A simple first connection.

When you submit a kitchen or bathroom inquiry, Nova Build uses your name, contact details, city, project description and optional timing to review and respond to your request. Inquiries are processed by our website host, Netlify, and stored in our existing Nova Leads system using Supabase. Please do not include sensitive documents or financial information.

Call and text links open your device’s phone or messaging application. Nothing is sent automatically. This website does not record calls or store messages; information you choose to send by text is received by Nova Build through its messaging service. Your provider’s normal terms and charges apply.

If you have a privacy question, contact Nova Build at (206) 699-9000.

With your optional consent, the form also saves campaign parameters from the page link to help us understand inquiry sources. These parameters are not proof of an advertising click. The form sets no advertising cookies. Your browser session stores only a random submission reference to prevent duplicate retries, not your contact details. Optional Google Analytics is off until you allow it using Analytics preferences at the bottom of the page. When allowed, Google receives page visits and contact-action events and uses analytics cookies. We do not send names, contact details, project descriptions, or full page-link parameters to Google Analytics. You can turn analytics off at any time using the same control. This preference is saved in your browser. Hosting providers may process technical information for security and operations. Call us for questions about access, correction or deletion of an inquiry.
